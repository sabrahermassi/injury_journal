# Vision

## Vision Statement

To empower people with chronic injuries to confidently navigate their healthcare journey by providing a single place to organize, understand, and communicate their medical history.

---

## Mission Statement

Build a secure and intuitive web application that enables users to record injuries, track symptoms and treatments, organize medical events, and create a clear timeline they can share with healthcare professionals.

---

## Product Principles

### 1. Organize, Don't Diagnose

The application helps users organize and understand their healthcare journey. It does not diagnose medical conditions, recommend treatments, or replace healthcare professionals.

### 2. Privacy First

Medical information is highly personal. User data should be handled securely and users should always remain in control of their own information.

### 3. Simplicity Over Complexity

The application should be easy to use, allowing users to focus on documenting their health rather than learning complicated software.

### 4. Accuracy and Trust

The system should faithfully record the user's information without altering or interpreting medical data. It serves as a reliable personal health journal.

### 5. Build Real Value First

Every feature should solve a genuine user problem. New technologies or advanced features should only be introduced when they clearly improve the user experience.

---

## Success Criteria

The first version of the application will be considered successful if a user can:

- Create an account and securely log in.
- Create one or more injury profiles.
- Record symptoms over time.
- Record treatments and rehabilitation activities.
- Record medical visits, tests, and procedures.
- View their complete medical journey as a chronological timeline.
- Return later and continue updating their information without losing previous records.

---

## Out of Scope (Version 1)

The MVP will **not**:

- Diagnose medical conditions.
- Recommend medications or treatments.
- Replace healthcare professionals.
- Predict recovery outcomes.
- Book medical appointments.
- Search for healthcare providers.
- Generate AI medical advice.
- Integrate with hospitals or electronic health record systems.

These capabilities may be explored in future versions but are intentionally excluded from the initial release to keep the product focused and achievable.

---

## Long-Term Vision

The long-term goal is to evolve Injury Journal from a personal tracking application into a comprehensive healthcare navigation platform that helps patients better understand their medical journey, communicate effectively with healthcare professionals, and make more informed healthcare decisions while always keeping medical decision-making in the hands of qualified professionals.
