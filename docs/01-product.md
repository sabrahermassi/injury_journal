# Product Overview

## Overview

Injury Journal helps users organize their injury history, symptoms, treatments, and medical events in one place.

The application focuses on documentation and communication with healthcare professionals, not diagnosis or medical advice.

---

## Problem

People with chronic injuries often have information spread across different providers, reports, and notes. This makes it difficult to remember previous symptoms, treatments, and medical events.

Injury Journal provides a structured timeline to keep this information organized.

---

## Target Users

People managing long-term injuries who want to track:

- Injury history.
- Symptoms.
- Treatments.
- Medical visits.
- Recovery events.

---

## MVP Features

- User authentication.
- Injury profile management.
- Symptom tracking.
- Treatment tracking.
- Medical visit tracking.
- Injury timeline view.
- Secure personal data management.

---

## Out of Scope

The MVP does not include:

- Medical diagnosis.
- Treatment recommendations.
- AI medical advice.
- Appointment booking.
- Healthcare provider search.
- Healthcare system integrations.
